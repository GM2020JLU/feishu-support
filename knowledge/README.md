# Optional knowledge authoring

The public source candidate includes a generic article template, but no
operational article corpus, embeddings, model weights, or evaluation data.
Those materials require their own rights, scope and access review before
redistribution. Their absence does not affect the software-only web quickstart.

Copy `templates/article.md` to a private article directory and fill in
claim-level sources, validation evidence, visibility and reviewer approval.
Do not publish raw chats, mail, logs or internal source snapshots. A template
or synthetic test is not an approved answer or an automatic-reply grant.
